Technical Architecture

TAN-1 ingests CGM data via the Dexcom G6/G7 API, computes rolling features (30/60/120 min averages, rate of change, time-in-range), and runs a gradient-boosted risk classifier. Alerts dispatch through a multi-tier escalation pipeline: push → SMS → phone call.

The Problem

37.3 million Americans have diabetes. Most caregivers can't read a CGM graph.

Continuous glucose monitors generate 288 data points per day. Caregivers receive raw numbers they can't interpret, leading to alert fatigue and missed critical events. The gap between clinical data and human understanding costs lives.

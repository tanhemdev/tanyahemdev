Technical Architecture

LyricSync connects via Spotify Web API OAuth, polls playback state at 100ms intervals, and maps timestamps to a custom contextual translation pipeline. Poetry stays poetry. Slang stays slang.

Connect Your Spotify

LyricSync uses the Spotify Web API to detect what you're playing in real time and sync translations to your music. You'll need a Spotify Premium account.

To enable live mode, register a Spotify app at developer.spotify.com, set the redirect URI to this page's URL, and paste your Client ID below.

The Problem

128 million people watched the Super Bowl halftime. Most had no idea what he was saying.

Bad Bunny performed the first all-Spanish halftime show. 77M of his US Spotify listeners don't speak Spanish. Google Translate gives you "Uncle asked me" for "Titi Me Preguntó." The music hits. The meaning doesn't.

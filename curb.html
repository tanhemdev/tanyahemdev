The Problem

Every May, perfectly good furniture goes to the landfill. Every August, freshmen buy it brand new.

US colleges generate massive move-out waste because there's no easy way to hand stuff off when everyone's moving at the same time. It's not a lack of caring — it's a logistics problem.

Technical Architecture

Curb is a mobile-first PWA geo-fenced to a 2-mile campus radius. Listings auto-expire in 72 hours. Real-time sync via Cloud Firestore, .edu email validation, and automatic image compression on upload.
